How to Register a Business Name with CAC (CAMA 2020 Procedure)

Published by LegalHub NG | Corporate Affairs Commission Guide

1. Introduction

Under the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA 2020), registering your business with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) provides legal recognition, protects your brand name, and enables opening formal corporate bank accounts in Nigeria.

2. Step-by-Step Registration Process

  1. Name Availability Search & Reservation:

    Log in to the CAC Company Registration Portal (CRP) and submit two proposed business names for reservation.

  2. Fill Registration Details:

    Provide principal place of business, nature of business activities, and details of proprietor(s).

  3. Upload Required Documents:

    Attach valid government-issued ID (NIN, Voter Card, Driver's License, or International Passport) and passport photographs.

  4. Payment of Statutory & Stamp Duty Fees:

    Pay statutory CAC filing fees and federal stamp duties online via Remita.

  5. Download Certificate of Registration:

    Once approved, download the electronic Business Name Certificate and Status Report (BN Form 01).

3. Post-Registration Obligations

After successful registration, business owners must file annual returns with the CAC to keep the business status active on the public search register.
