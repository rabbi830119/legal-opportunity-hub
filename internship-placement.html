    Commercial Litigation Internship Placement 2026

    Location: Hybrid / Virtual | Eligibility: Law Students & Recent Graduates

    About the Placement

    Applications are open for undergraduate law students and recent law graduates across Nigeria for the 2026 Commercial Litigation Internship. Selected interns will gain practical exposure to court processes, brief drafting, and legal research under experienced practitioners.

    Key Responsibilities

    • Conduct legal research on commercial disputes, arbitration, and corporate enforcement.
    • Assist in drafting court pleadings, affidavits, and legal opinions.
    • Attend virtual court proceedings and case review sessions.

    Requirements & Application Procedure

    Applicants must submit a single PDF containing their updated CV, a short cover letter, and a recent legal writing sample (max 1,000 words).
