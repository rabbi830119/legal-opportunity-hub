Nigerian Legal Review Essay Competition

Topic: CAMA 2020 Enforcement & Fintech Regulation | Cash Prizes & Publication

Overview

The Nigerian Legal Review invites scholarly essay submissions analyzing contemporary issues surrounding statutory enforcement of CAMA 2020 and emerging digital asset/Fintech regulations in Nigeria.

Submission Guidelines

  • Length: 2,500 – 4,000 words (including footnotes in OSCOLA style).
  • Eligibility: Open to law undergraduates, Law School students, and lawyers within 0–3 years post-call.
  • Format: MS Word document (.docx), double-spaced, 12pt Times New Roman.

Prizes

Winners receive cash awards, physical legal textbooks, and full publication in the peer-reviewed Nigerian Legal Review Journal.
