Public Interest & Legal Rights Fellowship

Duration: 6 Months (Remote) | Focus: Human Rights, Litigation Drafting & Policy

Program Description

A structured 6-month virtual fellowship designed to equip early-career legal practitioners and passionate law graduates with practical skills in fundamental rights enforcement, policy advocacy, and pro-bono defense strategies in Nigeria.

Fellowship Benefits

  • 1-on-1 mentorship with senior human rights litigators and legal scholars.
  • Practical workshops on filing enforcement applications in Nigerian courts.
  • Certificate of Completion and alumni network access.

How to Apply

Submit your statement of interest and CV detailing relevant advocacy experience or interest in public interest law.
